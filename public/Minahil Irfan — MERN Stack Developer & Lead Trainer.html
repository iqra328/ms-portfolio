About Me

Developer, Trainer & Mentor

I'm a results-driven MERN Stack Developer and Lead Trainer based in Karachi, Pakistan. With expertise spanning React.js, Next.js, Node.js, MongoDB, and CMS platforms like WordPress and Shopify, I build production-grade web applications that deliver real value.
